Privacy policy

LooksMatch · Version 1.0 · Last updated 20 October 2026

LooksMatch helps you compare how a dating profile photo is rated against a range set from your own photos. Most of what the app does happens on your phone. This policy explains what leaves your phone, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Who is responsible

The controller is StudioX Systems, Hofplein 18, 5223 LS 's-Hertogenbosch, the Netherlands, KvK 99844753. Contact for anything privacy-related: studioxsystems@gmail.com. We answer within one month.

2. What stays on your phone

  • Photos you pick (your own and screenshots of profiles). Faces are detected and scored on the phone. Screenshots of other people are never uploaded or stored by us.
  • Face comparison. To check that your range photos (or several photos of one profile) show the same person, the app computes a numeric description of each face. It exists only in the phone's memory during the check and is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • History thumbnails. Each check keeps a small face thumbnail in the app's private storage, excluded from phone backups, and deleted with the history entry, "Delete my data", "Reset app" or uninstalling.
  • Your date of birth. Used once to confirm you are 18 or older. Only the fact that you confirmed being an adult is synced.

We never upload photos or screenshots of other people, and we never store names, bios or links of profiles you check. We do not sell data, show ads, or use advertising IDs.

4. Photo sharing is optional

"Help improve LooksMatch" is off unless you say yes. Only face crops of your own range photos are uploaded, never full photos and never other people. Only the developer can view them, in the hosting dashboard. You can switch it off anytime in Profile; doing so deletes the photos you already shared.

5. The score

The score comes from an AI model trained on human ratings. It is a rough estimate for entertainment, may be wrong or biased (lighting, angle, expression, cultural taste), and is not a measure of anyone's worth. It is not used to make decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects. The app does not infer ethnicity, health, sexuality or other sensitive characteristics.

6. Who else processes data

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, storage): servers in Ireland (EU). Supabase Inc. is US-based; any access from outside the EU is covered by Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
  • RevenueCat (subscription management, USA): receives your user ID and purchase receipts. Covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework / Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Google (Google Play billing, optional Google sign-in) and Apple (App Store, optional Sign in with Apple) act under their own privacy policies.

We have data processing agreements with our processors. You can ask us for a copy of the transfer safeguards.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account data and derived numbers: until you delete them or your account.
  • Accounts never saved with an email or sign-in, that show no activity for 12 months: deleted automatically.
  • App events: 12 months.
  • Shared face crops: until you switch sharing off or delete your data, and at most 24 months.
  • Deleted data disappears from backups within 30 days.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google/Apple under their own (tax) obligations.

8. Your rights

You can access, correct, delete or export your data, object to processing based on legitimate interest, restrict processing, and withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect earlier processing). In the app: Profile → "Delete my data", or Profile → Manage account → "Delete account". Without the app: see Delete your account, or email studioxsystems@gmail.com.

You can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or the authority in your own EU country.

9. Adults only

LooksMatch is for people aged 18 and over. If you are under 18, the app stops at the age check and nothing is sent to our servers. If we learn that a minor's data reached us, we delete it.

10. Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Every user can only access their own rows. Shared photos sit in a private storage bucket that the app itself cannot read. Access to the dashboard is limited to the developer.

11. Changes

We will update this page when the app changes how it uses data, and tell you in the app about important changes.
