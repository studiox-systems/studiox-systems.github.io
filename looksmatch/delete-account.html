Delete your LooksMatch account

LooksMatch by StudioX Systems

In the app

  • Saved account (Google or Apple): Profile → Manage account → Delete account. This deletes your account, range, history and any shared photos from our server and your phone.
  • Not saved: Profile → Delete my data. This deletes your anonymous account, history, range and any shared photos from our server and your phone.

The app only says your data was deleted after our server confirms it.

Without the app

Email studioxsystems@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my LooksMatch account". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

If you never saved an account, your data is only linked to a random ID on your phone, so we can't find it from an email. It is deleted automatically after 12 months without activity.

What is deleted, and what isn't

  • Deleted: your account and sign-in details, your range, check results, feedback, app events and any face crops you shared. Copies in backups disappear within 30 days.
  • Not deleted by us: purchase records held by Google Play or Apple for their tax obligations.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a LooksMatch Pro subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions (or your Apple ID settings).
